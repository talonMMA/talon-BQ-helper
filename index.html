Hiring Manager Call — 定稿话术

主题：招聘经理（技术出身）初次通话的定稿话术
对象：Hiring Manager（首个实例 Antora · Liz Theurer，Senior Software Engineering Manager，此前是 Staff SWE）

1. Tell me about yourself（自我介绍）

定稿（2026-10-29 用户定；Claude 打样、用户逐句改；约 180 词，约 70 秒）

Thanks for taking the time, Liz.

So I'm a software engineer at Google. For the past four years, I've been a core developer on several high-precision GPS projects.

We deliver high-precision GPS to some really important teams.

Google Street View uses it to cut the long-tail errors in their maps by 20 to 30 percent.

Waymo uses it to build their maps, and it helps them map new cities faster as they expand their self-driving service.

Both are already in production. And since these are huge projects, that adds up to a real impact.

Our next customer is Wing. We want to bring high-precision GPS to their delivery drones.

On the engineering side, I'm mainly responsible for backend development and infrastructure, including performance optimization, large-scale monitoring, and our evaluation system. I also take new algorithms from research into production, and I've been driving AI adoption on my team.

Outside Google, I'm the full-stack engineer and architect on an AI system that helps mechanics diagnose vehicles. It's piloting in a fleet workshop right now.

So that's a bit about me. If there's a role on your team that's a good fit for me, I'd be happy to dig deeper.

2. What are you looking for in your next role?

用户口述整理（定稿 2026-10-29；约 75 词，约 30 秒）

I'm looking to move into AI. Not training the models themselves, but applied AI, things like AI agents, coding agents, and AI infrastructure.

On top of my main job, I've put a lot of time into this, both at work and on my own time, learning it and actually building with it. And I've found I really enjoy it.

So that's the main direction I'm looking at next.

3. Why are you leaving Google? / Why now?

用户口述整理（定稿 2026-10-29；约 55 词，约 20 秒）

It's pretty simple. I want to move from GPS work into AI. I think AI is the field worth focusing on for at least the next ten years.

Moving into an AI role inside Google, especially at Google DeepMind, is really competitive. Honestly, I'd rather build real hands-on experience at a smaller company, where I can own more of the work.

4. Why this company / this role?

用户口述整理（定稿 2026-10-29；第二句按公司换，下面是 Antora；约 65 词，约 25 秒）

I took a quick look at what your company is doing. You're building thermal batteries that turn cheap electricity into heat and power for industry and data centers. And this role builds the software products that run the business, and connects AI to the company's data. It lines up well with where I want to go next, which is focusing on AI.

I don't know much about your culture yet. But just from the business and the direction, it's what I'm looking for in my next job.

5. Do you need visa sponsorship?

Claude 按事实写（定稿 2026-10-29，用户要求一句话）

Yes, I'm on an H-1B, so I'd need a transfer, and since I've already been through the lottery, there's no new lottery involved.

6. What are your compensation expectations?

用户口述整理（定稿 2026-10-29）

I don't want to take a lower base than I have now, so I'm looking for a base of 200K, plus bonus on top.

On the total package, I'm pretty flexible.

7. Timeline: other processes, when could you start?

Claude 按事实写（定稿 2026-10-29，用户要求简单回答）

I'm in early conversations with a few other companies, nothing at the offer stage yet.

On start date, once there's an offer, the main step is filing the H-1B transfer. After that I can start, so usually within a few weeks.

8. Location: remote, hybrid, relocation?

用户口述整理（定稿 2026-10-29）

I'm based in San Francisco. I'd prefer remote, but I'm open to relocating if the role needs it.

9. 我反问招聘经理的问题

用户口述整理（定稿 2026-10-29）

What does the interview process look like from here?

That covers my main question, which was the interview process. I'd really like to talk with your engineers in the technical rounds, so I don't have anything else for now.
